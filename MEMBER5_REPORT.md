## Section 2 — Offline evolutionary weight optimisation

To complement the hand-designed evaluation function, an elitist real-valued genetic algorithm was used to optimise its seven coefficients. Each chromosome directly represents one weight vector, bounded between −10,000 and 10,000 to preserve the separation between heuristic and terminal scores. Fitness is measured through paired games under the official referee: every candidate plays both Attackers and Defenders from the same fixed opening, with two points for a win and one for a draw. The training pool contains the hand-set evaluator, GreedyBot, RandomBot, and optionally a previous champion. Tournament selection, arithmetic crossover, Gaussian mutation and elitism explore the parameter space while retaining promising solutions. Fixed-depth Alpha–Beta search makes evaluations affordable, and disjoint openings and opponents are reserved for validation.
